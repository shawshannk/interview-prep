Reading pages, not question banks: the basics each bank assumes, both technology-agnostic and specific to the .NET, Node, AWS and AI stack. Skim them first if a topic feels rusty, and keep the Quick-Revision Sheet for the night before.

Study order

Leadership questions and questions about your own projects come up in every TL/EM loop, so prepare your stories first. DSA is last because many EM loops skip it; check your target companies.

  1. 01
    Leadership & Behavioral

    Build 10–12 true stories. Write B050, B089 and B090 in your own words.

  2. 02
    System Design Drills

    Practise S030 (enterprise RAG) and S028 (group chat) aloud with a timer.

  3. 03
    Architecture & Distributed Systems

    Lead questions first; they test judgement interviewers weigh most for senior roles.

  4. 04
    AI & GenAI

    Often the differentiator. G087 (defending a portfolio project) and G086 (the "50% faster" pushback).

  5. 05
    .NET Backend and Cloud & DevOps

    Core stack depth. Expect internals questions on async, EF Core, ECS and IAM.

  6. 06
    LLD & Machine Coding

    Timed practice: L014, L025, L022. Compile each solution in a real project.

  7. 07
    Data Layer, Security, Testing & Observability, Node & NestJS, Frontend

    Breadth. Review by filter: Advanced and Lead first, Basic only to fill gaps.

  8. 08
    DSA Refresher

    Only if your targets run DSA rounds. Follow the 6-week plan in D002.
